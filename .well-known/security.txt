# Disclosure policy
#
# For vulnerabilities discovered in my clients' systems during an authorized
# engagement, findings are reported directly to the client under the signed
# rules of engagement. This contact is for out-of-scope findings only.
#
# For third-party products:
# - I follow coordinated disclosure.
# - I notify the vendor privately and allow a reasonable remediation window
#   (typically 90 days) before public disclosure.
# - I do not publish details of client systems without explicit written consent.
#
# This policy does not constitute permission to test any system I operate.
# Unauthorized testing is not authorized and will not be treated as research.
